Audience: deal teams and attorneys reviewing inbound NDAs against house rules. Action: open the demo / create an account. Proof: the redline itself (tracked changes on real NDA clause language from the rule set). No customers, metrics or testimonials exist.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a marked-up NDA. The hero headline is itself a tracked change: a counterparty clause struck through in redline, the firm's position inserted after it. It refuses the SaaS hero (badge, headline, two buttons, screenshot in a browser frame, three feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground, raised #0a0a0a panels, hairline white borders at 12% / 35%. Libre Caslon Display set in brushed-metal gradient for headlines (Caslon is the face of printed law); Inter Variable light for body and every working control. Redline #b04545 for deletions only; insertions underlined in metal. Pill outline buttons with a slowly wandering metallic border. Authored SVG outline sheets of paper, tilted in perspective, as the only ornament.

STORY: A reviewer sees an actual clause being redlined in the first second, understands rules come first and AI works inside them, sees the four rules the product applies, sees output is real Word tracked changes, then opens the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered wordmark that slides left on scroll; full-height black hero; headline "Mark up the NDA you <del>were sent</del> <ins>would have written.</ins>" at clamp(2.75rem,6vw,6rem), redline drawing in on load; one line of body; primary metal pill "Open the demo", secondary text link "Create account"; a tilted metallic paper outline drifting behind.

FORM: The erinys.ai design language, pinned by the owner. seed: none (pinned) "I want you to go through and do a detailed end to end analysis https://erinys.ai/ website design, all the effcts, animations everything. We are going to use this website design as the reference."
Signature interaction: pinned scroll sequence where three clauses of the review process light up in turn, the last one being redlined live; left § rail tracks sections. Working screens: tracked-change styling on every proposed change.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
